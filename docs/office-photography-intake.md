# Kgariya office photography intake

This guide is for preparing firm-approved office photographs for the website. Existing exterior imagery is to remain unchanged unless the firm expressly approves a replacement.

## Photographs to request

Please supply original, full-resolution files where possible. A recommended minimum is 2,000 pixels on the longest edge.

- Pretoria: reception, boardroom or meeting room, and a wide office view.
- Middelburg: reception or entrance, meeting room, and a representative workspace.
- Cape Town: reception or entrance, meeting room, and a representative workspace.
- Optional: one exterior or building-identification photograph per location, only if the firm approves its publication.

JPEG and PNG originals are suitable. Please do not send images copied from WhatsApp where a higher-quality original is available.

## Publication and privacy checks

Before publication, please confirm that Kgariya Attorneys Incorporated has permission to use each photograph on its public website.

Photographs should not reveal client names, matter files, privileged documents, confidential correspondence, access codes, computer screens containing personal information, visitor registers or other restricted information. Avoid identifiable people unless the firm has obtained their permission for public use.

Please identify the office represented by each photograph and supply a preferred caption where the context is not obvious.

## Website processing

Approved originals will be retained as source assets. Website derivatives can then be generated in WebP or AVIF, with responsive dimensions, compression and descriptive alternative text.

Suggested filenames:

- pretoria-reception-01.jpg
- pretoria-boardroom-01.jpg
- middelburg-reception-01.jpg
- middelburg-meeting-room-01.jpg
- cape-town-reception-01.jpg
- cape-town-workspace-01.jpg

The public directory should only contain assets that have passed the firm's publication and privacy checks. Location-specific maps, street addresses and branch contact details should be added separately, once the firm has approved those details.
